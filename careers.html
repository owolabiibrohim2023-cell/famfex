Site Civil & Structural Engineer

Urgent Opening Full-Time Owerri & Environs, Imo State B.Eng / HND Required

Responsibilities: Lead structural site execution, supervise rebar placement, concrete casting, ensure adherence to architectural blueprints, and enforce zero-defect quality control.

Requirements: 3+ years demonstrable site experience, proficiency with CAD/structural drawings, COREN/NSE certification is an advantage.

Senior Building & Construction Foreman

Full-Time Spibat Base & Field Sites 5+ Years Practical Experience

Responsibilities: Direct daily workforce of bricklayers, iron benders, and carpenters; manage daily material disbursements; maintain strict site discipline and morning safety briefings.

Requirements: Proven track record managing residential and civil building sites in Imo State, excellent leadership and problem-solving mindset.

Quantity Surveyor (QS) & Cost Estimator

Full-Time / Hybrid Owerri Office B.Sc / HND Quantity Surveying

Responsibilities: Prepare comprehensive Bills of Quantities (BOQ), conduct take-offs from architectural plans, negotiate supplier bulk rates, and prepare client valuation certificates.

Requirements: In-depth knowledge of current Nigerian construction material prices, NIQS membership is an added plus.

Heavy Equipment Operator (Excavator / Roller)

Requirements: Minimum 4 years heavy plant operations experience with basic preventive maintenance know-how.

Master Artisans (Iron Benders, Masons & Carpenters)

On-going Registration Site Roster Trade Test Certified / Apprentice Master
