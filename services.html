01. Structural Engineering

Residential & Commercial Building Construction

Famfex provides turnkey building solutions from soil testing, foundation excavation, raft/strip footing, concrete columns, deck casting, to superstructures.

Whether erecting a bespoke contemporary mansion in New Owerri, high-yield apartment complexes, shopping malls, or faith-based auditoriums, we handle all stages with licensed engineering scrutiny.

  • Raft, Pad & Pile Foundations for swampy or challenging terrain
  • High-tensile reinforcement steel bar bending & concrete casting
  • Turnkey roofing, aluminum casement windows, POP ceilings & Italian tiling

Typical Project Phases:

1
Architectural Drafting & Permits

Site topography, structural calculations & government approvals.

2
Substructure & Foundation

Trenching, hardcore compaction, DPC membrane & foundation casting.

3
Superstructure & Decking

Block laying, lintels, concrete beams and suspended slabs.

4
Finishing & Handover

Plastering, screeding, electrical wiring, plumbing & sanitaries.
